HOW TO APPLY THIS ZIP
======================
Extract directly into the ROOT of your project — paths mirror your
project structure, so every file replaces its existing counterpart at
the exact same path. Nothing here is a snippet; every file is a full,
ready-to-use replacement.

NOTHING WAS DELETED
--------------------
Every disabled feature's original implementation is still in the
codebase, just gated behind a stub/placeholder, with a comment
explaining exactly what to remove to bring it back. Withdraw (payout
of money already earned) and Boost (spending wallet balance on your
own listing) were left completely alone — neither is a licensing
concern.
